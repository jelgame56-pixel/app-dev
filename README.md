**Superman**  is a superhero created by writer **Jerry Siegel** and artist **Joe Shuster**. 
Superman is the archetypal superhero, he wears an outlandish costume, uses a codename, and fights evil and averts disasters with the aid of extraordinary abilities.

## Main Characters
**Superman** - a superhero that fights villains in the city.

**Supergirl** - Kara Zor-El is Superman's cousin and last survivor of Krypton's Argo City.

**Lex Luthor** - Superman's arch-nemesis, is one of the most brilliant men alive, whose hatred of "interfering aliens" pushes him down the path of a sociopath.

**Batman** - Bruce Wayne, who witnessed the murder of his billionaire parents as a child, swore to avenge their deaths.

### 🦸‍♂️ Themes 
Hero, fighting, and protecting the humanity
